Oompa Loompa layout for Herdr (personal build, 2026-11-07).
oompa-chrome, oompa-spaces, oompa-tabs, oompa-inbox and parts of oompa-agents are derived from
herdr-traveler by Frank Lin (https://github.com/franky5440-afk/herdr-traveler, Apache-2.0), itself
derived from Herdr's built-in mods. All art in these folders is new, drawn in code (art.py); none
of Traveler's images are used.
